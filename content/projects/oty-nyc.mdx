## The problem

Orthodox Tewahedo Youth NYC, a youth group I serve in, needed a home on the web. We needed to share our 
fellowship events, bi-weekly youth meetings and community service events with the broader online community.

## What we built

A website that answers the questions a young Orthodox Christian in New York actually has: what's
happening this week, where to go to church, and how to keep the faith in a busy city.

- **This week's events.** The home page opens on what's coming up: Learning Room sessions,
  fellowship gatherings, and service events, plus any upcoming fasts and feasts.
- **Who we are.** Our mission, and the three pillars the group is built on: **learning** (Church
  history, dogma, canons, and rites), **fellowship**, and **service**.
- **A live calendar.** A month view of every event. Tap a day to see times, locations, details,
  and any flyers attached.
- **Resources.** A directory of churches in the city with their service schedules, and a map of
  fasting-friendly places to eat during fasting seasons.
- **Ways to reach us.** Email and Instagram, one tap away.

The part we're proudest of is how it stays up to date. Everything event-related comes straight from
the group's Google Calendar. When organizers add an event there, it shows up on the site on its
own, with no code changes and no developer needed. The people running the group can keep the site
current with a tool they already use every week.
