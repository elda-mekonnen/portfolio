## The problem

A ROSCA (Rotating Savings and Credit Association) is an old idea. A group pays into a shared pot
on a schedule, and each period one member takes the whole pot until everyone has had a turn.
It works, but it usually lives across group chats, spreadsheets, and trust.

## What we built

Prosca puts the whole thing in one Android app. You can join public pods or run private ones.
An ML model scores each member's reputation from their past activity in the app. Payments run
automatically on a schedule, so nobody can drop out once a pod has started.

The backend is Node.js and Express on AWS (EC2, RDS MySQL, S3). The app is Kotlin with Retrofit.
Stripe handles payments, and a scikit-learn regression computes reputations.
